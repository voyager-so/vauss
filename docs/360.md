# 360 cameras

Vauss reads stitched 360 footage: the 2:1 equirectangular video or photos your camera's app exports
(Insta360, GoPro Max, Ricoh Theta, Qoocam and others).

```sh
vauss run walk360.mp4 --goal "walk-through of the garden" --driver fixed          # detected by itself
vauss run ./pano-frames --camera-model equirect --goal "..." --driver fixed      # or declared
```

## How it works

Each 360 frame is cut into 14 ordinary 90-degree views (four level, four tilted up, four tilted down, one
straight up, one straight down) that share one camera position. Cameras are solved with the views locked
together as a rig, and training, the viewer and every export then work as for any other run.

## The flags

| Flag | What it does |
|---|---|
| `--camera-model equirect` | Treat the input as 360 footage. Without it, Vauss detects 360 footage by itself: every frame exactly 2:1, at least 1920 px wide, and left and right edges that join (or 360 metadata in the video). Any other `--camera-model` turns detection off. |
| `--target-count N` | With 360 input, the number of 360 frames kept: default 100 (1,400 views), 20–250. |
| `--nadir-mask-deg D` | The area below the camera (you, the stick or the tripod) is ignored: a cone of D degrees around straight down, default 30, 0 turns it off. |

## What to expect

- About 100 frames by default. Frames are decoded at up to 4096 px wide; each view is a quarter of that
  width, at most 1024 px (960 px for 3840 × 1920 footage). `--max-edge` and `--fast`'s smaller frames
  do not apply to 360 input. Every view trains (`SPLAT_TRAIN_MAX_FRAMES` does not apply).
- The area right under the camera is not reconstructed.
- Faint errors can appear along the camera's stitch line, where its two lenses meet (usually the sides).
- Film without people walking through the shot: moving people are not masked out in 360 runs yet.
- Hold the camera upright. Tilted footage still works, but the area that is masked may then miss you.
- Photo folders are read in file-name order, as one walk (name them so they sort in capture order).

## What does not work yet

- Raw dual-fisheye files (`.insv`, GoPro `.360`): export a stitched video from the camera's app first.
- Bringing your own 360 camera poses (`--trust-poses` is refused for 360 input).
- `--pairs exhaustive`, `--preserve-all` and the Spirula backend (`--backend spirula`).

## Troubleshooting

**"360 footage detected" on ordinary video.** Some phones film 18:9 video, which is also exactly 2:1. If
the message appears for ordinary footage, add `--camera-model pinhole`.

**Not detected.** Add `--camera-model equirect`. The footage must be exactly 2:1 (for example 5760 × 2880).

See also [filming with a 360 camera](filming-guide.md#with-a-360-camera) and the 360 notes in
[outputs.md](outputs.md#360-runs).
