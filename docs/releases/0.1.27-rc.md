Vauss 0.1.27-rc makes camera solving for 360 captures 3–4× faster.

- **360 camera solve is 3–4× faster.** Before, each view of a panorama was matched against all 14 views of each neighbouring panorama, and most of those matches found nothing. Now Vauss first estimates how the camera turned between panoramas, then matches only the views that look in overlapping directions. On the OmniPhotos test scenes:
  - Temple3 (72 panoramas): camera solve went from 28.3 to 7.4 min, and the whole run from 31.9 to 10.9 min.
  - Ballintoy (94 panoramas): camera solve went from 38.0 to 11.4 min, and the whole run from 42.4 to 15.7 min.
  
  Every panorama still registers in one model, and quality on held-out views is within 0.16 dB of before. Where a turn can't be estimated reliably, Vauss falls back to matching all view pairs for those panoramas. `SPLAT_PANO_PAIRS=sequential` restores the old matcher.

Known limitations: otherwise unchanged from 0.1.26-rc.
