Vauss 0.1.26-rc fixes shot planning for 360 captures.

- **`vauss shot` risk scores are correct for 360 runs.** For captures made from 360 (equirectangular) footage, `risk.json` used to rate a frame as high risk whenever the camera turned away from each panorama's forward view, so a camera sitting exactly on the capture path scored 2.0 (high) when turned 90°. A panorama sees every direction, so risk for 360 rig captures now depends only on the distance from the nearest panorama centre. Frames that really leave the captured area are still flagged, and good shots in any direction are no longer. Ordinary (pinhole) captures are scored exactly as before.

Known limitations: unchanged from 0.1.25-rc. Camera solving for 360 input is still slow (31–40 minutes for 72–94 panoramas on a 32-core machine); a speed-up is tracked.
