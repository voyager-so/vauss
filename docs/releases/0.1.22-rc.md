Vauss 0.1.22-rc improves reconstruction safety, frame inclusion and calibrated exports.

- Geometry health now checks observed-depth scale, observation-graph support and corroborated translation jumps. Incoherent high-registration solves reject before training and can enter learned-pose rescue. Failed179 is a rejecting fixture; corrected241 is a passing fixture.
- `--preserve-all` keeps input inclusion explicit through extraction, SfM and training, with truthful input/selected/dropped/registered/train/heldout counts. Pre-extracted video can declare its provenance; explicit anisotropic calibration and matcher overrides remain authoritative.
- Ordered video matching uses chronological sequential matching with existing loop-closure/fallback machinery, without silently downsampling preserved input.
- Per-view QA retains names and flags poor views rather than relying on aggregate scores. Exact-name native diagnostics are available.
- Ordinary Blender/import workflows receive a complete scene even when SPZ needs viewer-specific sidecars. Exported cameras retain exact unequal focal lengths, principal points, roll and transforms. Source/output identity guards prevent destructive aliasing.

Known visual investigation: the corrected241 demo model still has poor exact source-camera views100/101/103/104. Native original/701-candidate diagnostic ablation confirms localized failure; removing the projected near-camera group alone does not cure it and can worsen a view. This release detects/flags the affected views; it does not claim a verified cure or safe automatic pruning. A controlled pose-refinement A/B remains an open investigation.

The separate custom visual-lab film renderer is outside Vauss scope. Native/export representation parity is distinguished from Blender/web rasterizer equivalence. Original upstream licence notices are retained.
