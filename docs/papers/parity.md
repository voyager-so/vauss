# Historical benchmark and parity summary

These results were recorded through **24 October 2026**. They describe the tested recipes and
hardware, not a guarantee for every scene or the current release. Current launch measurements are
reported separately in the repository README.

The tables retain negative results, coverage limitations, comparator recipes and uncertainty.
**AHEAD / PARITY / BEHIND** are the original study verdicts under each row's protocol.
**PROVISIONAL**, **EXPLORATORY** and **UNVERIFIED** results do not establish a general advantage.
Historical `HEADLINE` classifications describe the study's registration/review status, not an
endorsement of using every row as a current marketing claim. LPIPS means canonical VGG with
normalization unless the row states otherwise. “By us” denotes a measured baseline; published
numbers are author-reported unless explicitly reproduced.

Every `verified/...` citation below resolves under [tools/verified/](tools/verified/). Records
are retained with their cited record dependencies. Numerical results, checksums, protocols and
limitations are preserved. Internal source revisions use descriptive `historical-build-NNN` labels;
these are study identifiers, not commits in this public repository. Raw private run directories,
internal planning documents and unpublished paper drafts are not shipped.
